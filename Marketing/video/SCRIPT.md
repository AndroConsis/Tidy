# Tidy — video scripts

Two cuts from one story. The **App Store preview** has to follow Apple's
rules, so it shows only real Tidy footage with captions. The **story video**
is a fully animated piece for the website and social media.

Neither video mentions or shows the GitHub repository.

---

## 1. App Store preview — "Space back in one click" (≈26 s)

Apple's rules for macOS app previews:

- 15–30 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 (.mov/.mp4), up to 500 MB.
- Guideline 2.3.4: only screen captures of the app itself. Text overlays and
  narration are allowed; unrelated animation is not.
- No claims the app can't back up (e.g. "makes your Mac run cooler").
- Plays muted by default, so every beat must work without sound.

| Time | On screen (real Tidy footage) | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Overview on a nearly full Mac: 8 GB free, 60 GB reclaimable, amber bar | **Mac almost full?** |
| 3–8 s | Xcode section — runtimes, DerivedData, device support, each sized | **Tidy finds what's really taking space** |
| 8–12 s | Dev Caches — npm, Homebrew, Gradle, CocoaPods with SAFE / REVIEW badges | **Every item marked Safe or Review** |
| 12–18 s | Back to Overview; cursor clicks **Clean all safe items · 31 GB**; the storage bar animates and free space jumps | **31 GB back in one click** |
| 18–22 s | System section — Photos Library marked MANUAL, "Tidy never deletes this itself" | **Your photos and videos stay untouched** |
| 22–26 s | Menu bar popover, then end card with the app icon | **Tidy — Mac Storage Cleaner** |

## 2. Story video — "Keep your cool" (≈26 s)

A flat, colourful animated short for the landing page, social posts and ads.
The heat is a visual metaphor for "storage-full stress", not a technical
claim, so the copy never says Tidy cools the Mac.

| Time | Scene | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | A laptop character sweats and glows red; heat waves rise; its screen shows a 97% full storage bar and a warning. Its owner sits beside it, stressed. | **Storage almost full…** |
| 5–9 s | Photo and video thumbnails drift towards a trash can — the tempting fix — and get stopped with a big ✕. | **Don't delete your memories.** |
| 9–15 s | The Tidy icon swoops in with sparkles and sweeps up bubbles labelled *Xcode caches 18 GB*, *npm cache 2 GB*, *Old installers 3 GB*, *App caches 4 GB*. The storage bar drains to 45%. The photos stay. | **Tidy clears the junk you'll never miss.** |
| 15–21 s | The laptop turns teal and smiles; the heat waves become a gentle breeze. Its owner relaxes with a coffee. | **Space back. Memories kept.** |
| 21–26 s | End card: the Tidy icon and name. | **Tidy — Mac Storage Cleaner** / *Available on the Mac App Store* / prateekrathore.com/tidy |

Voice-over (optional, ~55 words, calm and friendly):

> Is your Mac almost full? Before you start deleting photos and videos…
> meet Tidy. Tidy finds the caches, build data and old installers you'll never
> miss — and clears them in one click. Everything goes to the Trash first, and
> your memories stay right where they are. Space back. Memories kept.
> Tidy, on the Mac App Store.
